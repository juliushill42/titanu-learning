# Life Admin Copilot

This deliverable is a finished static landing page for the Life Admin Copilot consumer product.

## Included
- `life-admin-copilot.html` — complete responsive landing page
- Built-in light and dark mode
- Consumer positioning, feature framing, FAQ, and one-time purchase pricing

## Deployment
1. Upload `life-admin-copilot.html` to your host.
2. Rename it to `index.html` if your hosting service expects that name.
3. Replace the mailto purchase link with your checkout link when payment is live.

## Product definition
Life Admin Copilot is a calm, consumer-ready life-admin app for reminders, recurring tasks, renewals, forms, scheduling, follow-ups, and household organization.
